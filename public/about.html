About MHS Today

MHS Today is made by students, for Mukwonago High School students: today's day letter and your classes, the lunch menu, events, clubs and sports, and where your friends are.

MHS Today is an independent project made by students. It is not run by, part of, or endorsed by Mukwonago High School or the Mukwonago Area School District. It runs on its own server, not the school's. If the school or district ever needs to look into something on the site (for example, bullying), we let them.
