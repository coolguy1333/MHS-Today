About MHS Today

MHS Today is a free website for Mukwonago High School: today's day letter and bell schedule with your own classes in it, the lunch menu, events, clubs, and where your friends are. It works on Chromebooks, phones and computers, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

An independent project made by students. It is not run by, part of, or endorsed by Mukwonago High School or the Mukwonago Area School District, and it runs on its own server, not the school's. If the school or district ever needs to look into something on the site (for example, bullying), we let them.

For students

  • Today: the day letter, the bell schedule and what's happening now, with your own classes, rooms and lunch. Simple mode shows a big clock and the time left.
  • My classes: enter each class once by color block, or paste your schedule from Infinite Campus.
  • Assignments: tests, quizzes and assignments classmates add for your classes, and study sessions.
  • Events, Calendar and Groups: school events, clubs and sports, all on one calendar you can add to Google Calendar.
  • Friends: see where your friends are and who shares your lunch. You choose what they see.
  • Lunch: the day's menu and your lunch time.

For teachers

  • Pick Teacher when you first sign in.
  • Today shows your own classes through the day.
  • Lesson plans: write what each class is doing each day; your students see it next to the class.
  • Groups: clubs can invite you to be their advisor and run the group with its student leaders.

How the day letter works

Every school day is Day A, B, C or D, moving forward by one each school day (days off are skipped). Each class belongs to a color block, and the day letter decides which hour each color meets in, following the MHS Daily Schedule. Advisory, early release and special days like MukReady have their own bell times.

Your privacy

You sign in with your school Google account; there are no passwords here. We keep what you add so the site works, never sell it and show no ads. You can download everything we keep about you, or delete your account, at any time. Read the full Privacy & Terms.

Questions, ideas or something broken?

Use the Feedback page; the people who run MHS Today answer there. Schedules, day letters and lunch menus can be wrong or change, so check official school sources for anything important.
