      Graduation

      On graduation day, seniors' accounts (12th grade, not admins) are archived: they can't sign in and drop out of search and friends lists, and they're deleted 90 days later unless you restore one. Everyone else moves up a grade. The grade is what each student picked on their profile.

      Announcements

      A banner at the top of every page from the start date to the end date (a snow day, spirit week). People can close it on their device.

      Countdowns

      Shown on Today until the date passes (finals, prom, winter break…).

      Blocked words

      A built-in filter already catches common swear words and disguises like sh1t. Add more here: they're blocked in names, bios, events, study sessions, groups, posts and polls. Whole words only.
