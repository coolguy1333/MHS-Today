Before you start

Privacy & Terms

Last updated October 20, 2026.

Privacy: what we keep and why

When you sign in with Google

We get your name, your school email address and an ID number from Google. We never see or store your Google password.

What you add

  • Your profile: display name, avatar, and optionally pronouns, grade, bio and interests.
  • Your classes, teachers, rooms and lunch.
  • Friends, events, sign-ups, groups, posts, polls, study sessions and feedback.

Who sees it

  • Your friends see your classes and lunch, and you choose for each friend what they see (or turn it off in Profile → Privacy).
  • People in a group see its posts; events are seen by who you pick (everyone, a group, or friends you invite).
  • Classmates in the same class see study sessions for that class.
  • Admins (the people who run MHS Today) can see accounts and posts so they can keep the site safe.

The record of activity

MHS Today keeps a permanent record of sign-ins and changes made on the site (who did what and when, and the network address it came from). Only the site's owners can see it, and it is kept even after an account is deleted. If Mukwonago High School or the school district needs to look into something (for example, bullying or a safety concern), the owners can let them see the relevant part of this record and of the site.

What we don't do

No ads, no selling your information, and no tracking. Apart from letting the school look into something when needed (above), your information isn't shared with anyone. It is stored on MHS Today's own server, not the school's.

Your choices

  • Download my data: everything the site keeps about you, as a file.
  • Delete my account: removes your profile, classes, friends, events and posts. (The record of activity above stays.)

Terms: using MHS Today

MHS Today is an independent project made by students. It is not run by, part of, or endorsed by Mukwonago High School or the Mukwonago Area School District.

  • Use your own school Google account, and only for school-related things.
  • Treat it like school: follow the student code of conduct. Be kind: no bullying, threats, hate, or inappropriate content.
  • Don't post other people's private information.
  • Admins can remove posts and events, and limit or remove accounts that break these rules.
  • Schedules, day letters and lunch menus can be wrong or change. Check official school sources for anything important.
  • MHS Today is an independent student project, offered as is. It may change or stop.
